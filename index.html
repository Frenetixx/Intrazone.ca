FAQ

Good questions. Straight answers.

Don’t see yours? Ask me directly at jason@intrazone.ca.

How much does a website cost?

Web Design is $79 CAD a month with a 12-month commitment ($948 total), or $599 one-time if you pay upfront. If you prefer to pay once, website packages are $599 for a one-page Starter site, $1,299 for a Business site of up to five pages, and $4,999 for a Premium custom-designed site of up to eight pages. Domain registration, paid software, advertising spend and applicable taxes are additional.

Do you only work with businesses in Kitchener-Waterloo?

I’m based in Kitchener-Waterloo and love working with local organizations across Waterloo Region, but I’m also available for remote projects anywhere in Canada.

Can I start with just one service?

Yes. You can start with whatever matters most right now, like a website or a month of social content, and add more later. Every service has a listed price and scope.

Do you work with churches, ministries and nonprofits?

Yes. Since 2022 I’ve supported Fountain of Hope Ministries & Healing Centre in Kitchener with content, graphics, YouTube and SEO, and secured its Google Ad Grant, worth up to $10,000 USD a month in Google search advertising.

Do you guarantee Google rankings?

No. No one can honestly guarantee rankings or traffic. Local SEO work focuses on practical improvements: clearer page titles and content, your Google Business Profile, and brief reporting so you can see what changed.

Can you get my business into ChatGPT or Google’s AI answers?

AI search tools like Google’s AI Overviews and ChatGPT pull from websites they can read and trust. I make your site and business information clear, accurate and easy for them to understand, which gives you a better chance of being mentioned. No one can guarantee AI mentions, but the same work also helps your regular Google results.

What isn’t included in the prices?

Domain registration, hosting, paid software, subscriptions, stock assets, advertising spend and applicable taxes are additional. Ecommerce and custom applications require separate quotes, and anything beyond a listed scope is quoted separately.

How do we get started?

Send the enquiry form or call (519) 465-8919. I’ll talk with you about your goals, then send a written quote with a clear scope. Work starts once you approve it.
